Section 1: Roads and Transport

The Roads and Transport program has an operating and capital budget of $14.2 million for the 2026-27 financial year. The main focus this year is resurfacing 86 km of local roads.

The flagship project is the Kestrel River bridge upgrade, which is scheduled to finish by March 2027. The program is led by Priya Nair, who reports to the Chief Executive Officer.

Council consulted residents through workshops, online surveys and drop-in sessions across the region before finalising these priorities. 
Delivery will be staged so that disruption to residents and businesses is kept to a minimum, with works scheduled outside peak periods where possible. 
Progress will be reported to Council quarterly, and any variation of more than ten percent to the allocated budget must be approved by resolution. 
The program aligns with the regional strategy adopted last year and draws on state co-funding where eligible.
